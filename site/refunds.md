# Refund policy

Last updated {{ legal.updated }}.

Payments for the Moatline cloud are processed by Stripe, the merchant of record for our sales (Stripe Managed Payments). Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

## 14-day refund

If you are not happy with the Service, you can ask for a full refund within **14 days** of your first payment for a plan — no reasons needed.

## After 14 days

Plans are monthly and can be cancelled at any time under Settings → Billing; the plan then ends at the end of the month already paid for, and nothing further is charged. Payments for past periods are not refunded, except where the Service was unavailable for a substantial part of the period or the law requires it.

## Switching plans

When you move to a smaller plan, the unused part of the current period is credited against the next payments; when you move to a bigger one, the difference is charged for the rest of the period.

## Right of withdrawal (consumers in the EU)

As a consumer in the European Union you have the right to withdraw from the contract within 14 days without giving a reason. The 14-day refund above covers this right in full.

## How to ask

Write to {{ legal.email }} with the email address of your account, or reply to the receipt Stripe sent you. Refunds are usually processed within a few days.
